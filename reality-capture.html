High-fidelity 3D scanning for existing buildings and complex sites.

We capture accurate existing conditions for buildings and sites across Greece using 3D laser scanning, LiDAR, photogrammetry and drone capture — delivering reliable spatial data for architects, engineers, contractors and property owners.

Why 3D scanning

Accurate existing-condition data, captured once.

Instead of relying on outdated drawings, manual measurements and fragmented site photos, you get an accurate digital record of existing conditions that can be reused across design, renovation, engineering and construction.

Capture once, reuse everywhere

A single scan session provides geometry and imagery that can feed architects, engineers, contractors and facility managers—all from the same source of truth.

Ideal for renovations, existing buildings and complex sites.

Reduce site visits

Explore the space virtually, measure distances, areas and heights after the fact. No more repeated trips just to “double-check” dimensions.

Save time for teams and clients.

Design with confidence

Starting from accurate geometry minimizes clashes, RFIs and change orders. What we build in 3D matches what exists on site.

Better coordination, fewer surprises.

    Deliverables

      Process

      From existing conditions to usable project data.

      We combine terrestrial 3D laser scanning, LiDAR, photography and drones to capture existing conditions, then register, clean and structure the data in-house for point-cloud, CAD, Scan-to-BIM and 3D-model deliverables.

      Capture

      01
      Pre-scan planning

      We review drawings (if available), define scope and level of detail, and plan stations and drone flight paths.

      02
      On-site scanning

      We scan interiors and exteriors with LiDAR, and supplement with high-resolution photography and aerial coverage where relevant.

      03
      Quality checks

      Before leaving site, we verify coverage, fill any gaps and confirm critical areas have been captured.

      Processing & delivery

      Point Cloud • CAD • Scan-to-BIM • 3D Models

      04
      Registration & cleaning

      Scan data is aligned, denoised and structured in-house. We remove stray geometry and optimize files for performance.

      05
      Data formats

      We can deliver point clouds (E57, RCP, LAS, etc.), meshes or models compatible with your preferred tools.

      06
      Optional modeling

      Where needed, we can convert the scan into measured drawings, a clean 3D model or BIM geometry through a Scan-to-BIM workflow as a separate scope.
